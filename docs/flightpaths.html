Each line is one aircraft's track over a single day, reconstructed from 5-second ADS-B Exchange snapshots within the chosen radius of the city centre. Files are large (tens of MB), so the first load of a city can take a moment.

The noise map turns the same data into estimated Day-Night Average Sound Levels for the whole planet.
